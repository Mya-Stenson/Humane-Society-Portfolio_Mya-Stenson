03 / A little about me

Creative work,
with a soft spot.

I'm a Media Arts graduate who likes making things that feel considered, useful, and human.

Add your photo as images/my-dogs.jpg
the best kind of coworkers ✳

I studied Media Arts at the University of Montana, where I explored digital design, web development, and visual communication. I like moving between the visual and practical sides of a project: how it looks, how it works, and how clearly it communicates.

Outside of design, animal rescue is close to my heart. I'm drawn to the Humane Society because its work matters in a very tangible way. I'd love to bring my creative skills, curiosity, and care for the details to work that supports animals and the people who help them.
